\section*{Public-v1 release note}

The diagnostic protocol was frozen on 10 July 2026 at 09:33 UTC using only the LeWM seed-3072 Gaussian calibration split. The immutable protocol file has SHA-256
\codebrk{edcb801c3da388e673c9b55d706a558aa01da7a281fc151e52e1cda566045a21}.
All held-out LeWM, blur/resize, and failed target-view results were evaluated without threshold retuning.

This release reports three independently trained seeds for both LeWM and PLDM. Each checkpoint is evaluated with three additional seeds, which quantify conditional evaluation variability rather than training-run replication. Blur/resize results are a fixed-strength strongest-severity boundary test, not a corruption benchmark. The sharp certificate applies to one sampled ordered candidate pool, and the JVP analysis is local. Component and SMPR controls do not establish that a long horizon, correct-action conditioning, task labels, or four-task oracle semantics are empirically necessary.

The repository contains code, aggregate evaluation files, rendering scripts, and data/checkpoint pointers for this revision. Artifact hashes and statistical-unit semantics are recorded in \code{DATA\_MANIFEST.md}; the release does not claim that every raw training dataset or checkpoint is redistributed.

\section*{v2 revision note (internal)}

The v2 revision is presentational; no experiment was rerun and no threshold,
protocol constant, or evaluation split changed. The checkpoint readouts were
renamed for the paper text only: Action-Conditioned Trajectory Radius (ATR)
is now the Invariance Radius (IR), and Selective Margin Pass Rate (SMPR),
briefly termed Distinguishability Rate (DR), is now the Separation Rate (SR);
thresholds $t_R/t_M$ are now $t_{\mathrm{IR}}/t_{\mathrm{SR}}$. Released
result files, column names, and some internal identifiers may still carry the
v1 or former-DR names and map one-to-one under this renaming.

Other v2 changes: the title changed from ``Action-Conditioned Predictive
Diagnostics for JEPA World Models'' to ``Diagnosing JEPA World Models with
Action-Conditioned Predictive Consistency''; the abstract was rewritten
(problem-first, bisimulation as a one-sentence transition, no protocol
jargon); Figure~1 was redesigned as a three-block fragile/robust contrast
(\code{assets/paper1\_figs/fig\_acpc\_ir\_sr\_overview.pdf}, generated by
\code{paper1/scripts/plot\_acpc\_ir\_sr\_overview.py}); related work was
expanded (self-predictive RL, robust world-model training, decision-aware
diagnostics, adversarial evaluations, robustness certificates); the
local-geometry case study moved from the method section into the
experiments; and near-tautological screening claims were removed from the
abstract and discussion. The v1 note above retains v1 terminology (SMPR)
for historical accuracy.
